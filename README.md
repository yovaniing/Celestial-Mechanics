# Celestial-Mechanics
Software in C++ and Python to simulate the behavior of the aerospace environment.

Celestial mechanics seeks to determine and predict the trajectories of celestial bodies in space. This problem has been studied scientifically since Newton's time, but most cases remain unsolved. In fact, only general solutions are known when there are two bodies in the system, and some partial solutions with three, four, and five bodies. This study is very interesting because it can be approached from a purer perspective, finding analytical solutions, or with numerical approximations, where algorithms and computational capabilities are essential.

One question that remains unanswered, and is very important, is whether the solar system in which we live is stable or whether everything will one day get out of control. For example, could the Moon fall on our heads? We still don't know for sure.
